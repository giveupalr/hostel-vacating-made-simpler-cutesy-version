Steps, in order

    Good to know

    Get signatures earlyThe HOD is very busy after FAT or near the end of term. You can collect signatures early, but you can only upload to VTOP 2-3 days before you vacate.
    Order mattersEverything happens in the order above. You can't mix up the steps.
    Leaving campus afterwardsOnce vacating is complete, you'll need a letter from the warden to go out of campus.
    Non-CDC studentsInstead of the congratulations mail, we assume the internship approval on VTOP works. We're not sure how the rest of the non-CDC process goes, so good luck!
